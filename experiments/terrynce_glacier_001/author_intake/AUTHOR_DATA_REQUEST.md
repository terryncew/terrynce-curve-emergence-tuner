# Missing derived-data specification

TERRYNCE-GLACIER-001 cannot run its frozen headline comparison from the current
public archive. This document records the observations that would change that
status. It is a public data specification, not private correspondence.

## Required measurements

1. The centre-line displacement or velocity observations underlying the final
   Bukadaban East points in Figure 6 of Kääb et al., especially the 30–31
   October and 31 October–1 November 2022 intervals.
2. The along-flow order or profile distance of the sampled points, with the
   lowermost pinned-terminus section identified for East and the corresponding
   profile definition for West.
3. Dated terminus-position measurements supporting the stable East terminus
   and the approximately 500 m West advance.
4. Identifiers and comparable time series, if already derived, for other
   surge-type glaciers on the Xinqingfeng ice cap that accelerated without
   detaching.
5. A license or permission statement covering analysis and any allowed
   redistribution.

Raw PlanetScope imagery is outside the experiment contract. Only derived
numeric measurements are needed.
