# Draft request for derived Bukadaban measurements

**To:** Andreas Kääb (`kaeaeb@geo.uio.no`)  
**Subject:** Derived Bukadaban velocity data underlying Figure 6

Dear Professor Kääb,

I am conducting an independent, prefix-only replication of the warning signal
described in *Recent giant detachment of a glacier on the Tibetan plateau
provoked by its frozen tongue*.

I verified the public Zenodo v1 archive at DOI
`10.5281/zenodo.17754687` (MD5
`cf418a1dc5f062e2ba2c2f98008f3e40`). Its `disp.zip` contains 30 Bukadaban
East intervals ending on 16 October 2022 and 12 Bukadaban West intervals. I
could not find the PlanetScope-derived 30–31 October and 31 October–1 November
measurements reported in the paper, or the spatial labels needed to reproduce
the upstream-versus-terminus comparison.

Would you be willing to share the following **derived numeric measurements**?

1. The centre-line displacement or velocity observations underlying the final
   Bukadaban East points in Figure 6, especially 30–31 October and 31
   October–1 November 2022.
2. The along-flow order or profile distance of the sampled points, with the
   lowermost pinned-terminus section identified for East and the corresponding
   profile definition for West.
3. The dated terminus-position measurements supporting the stable East
   terminus and the approximately 500 m West advance.
4. If already derived, identifiers or comparable time series for the other
   surge-type glaciers on the Xinqingfeng ice cap that accelerated without
   detaching.
5. The license or permission under which these derived values may be analyzed
   and, if allowed, redistributed with an open replication.

Raw PlanetScope imagery is neither requested nor required. I will preserve the
authors' values as a pinned source artifact, use only observations available at
each historical cutoff, and report a null result if the joint signal fails to
beat velocity-only and prospective power-law baselines at the same observed
false-alarm burden.

I can accept ordinary CSV files or adapt a supplied table. A machine-readable
schema is included in the public replication repository if useful.

Thank you for considering the request.

Terrynce White  
https://github.com/terryncew/terrynce-curve-emergence-tuner
